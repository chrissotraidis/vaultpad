# Third-party notices

VaultPad includes and modifies [Fallout 2 Community Edition](https://github.com/fallout2-ce/fallout2-ce) as the `engine` submodule. That engine is distributed under the Sustainable Use License in [`engine/LICENSE.md`](engine/LICENSE.md). VaultPad's iPad-specific engine modifications are recorded in the engine branch history.

The engine brings its own third-party dependencies and notices, including SDL, zlib, fpattern, and lodepng. Their source, license files, pinned revisions, and build definitions remain inside the engine submodule.

VaultPad does not include Fallout 2 game data. Users must import files from a legally purchased copy into their private app container.
